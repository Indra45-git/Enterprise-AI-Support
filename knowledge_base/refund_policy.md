# Enterprise Refund Policy

## 1. Refund Processing Timeline
Refunds are processed promptly once a returned item passes warehouse inspection:
- **Warehouse Inspection**: 1 to 2 business days after package receipt at our central depot.
- **Refund Initiation**: Within 24 hours of inspection approval.
- **Bank Settlement**: **3 to 5 business days** depending on your banking institution or payment gateway.

## 2. Refund Methods
All refunds are credited strictly to the **original payment method** used at checkout:
- **UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm)**: 24 to 48 hours settlement to the linked bank account.
- **Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay)**: 3 to 5 business days reflected on your card statement.
- **Net Banking**: 3 to 5 business days to the originating account.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Secure direct bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) upon customer providing verified IFSC and account details through a support ticket.

## 3. Human Approval & Security Governance
In accordance with zero-trust financial compliance:
- The AI Support Agent can determine return eligibility and initiate a refund request, but final disbursement is confirmed through verified human-in-the-loop audit controls.
- Any single refund exceeding ₹10,000 requires two-factor supervisor sign-off before settlement.

## 4. Non-Refundable Scenarios
- Products returned with physical tampering, missing original accessories, or mismatched serial numbers.
- Return requests submitted after the expiration of the 10-day return window.
- Orders where delivery was completed and items are marked non-returnable in the catalog.
