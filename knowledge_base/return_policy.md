# Enterprise Return Policy

## 1. 10-Day Return Window
Customers may initiate a return for any eligible item within **10 calendar days** of the confirmed delivery date. The return window begins on the day the carrier marks the order as "Delivered" and closes at midnight on the 10th day.

## 2. Item Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for a full return and refund:
- The product must be designated as **returnable** in the verified product catalog (non-returnable items are strictly exempt).
- The item must be unused, in its original condition, with all manufacturer packaging, barcodes, tags, manuals, and accessories intact.
- Electronic products must have serial numbers matching our warehouse records and all security seals undamaged.
- Hygiene-sensitive items, opened software, and customized products are non-returnable.

## 3. Return Procedure
1. **Eligibility Verification**: Use the AI Support Agent or Support Portal to verify your order ID and product SKU.
2. **Return Authorization**: Once verified, a return merchandise authorization (RMA) ticket is automatically generated.
3. **Courier Pickup**: Our logistics partner arranges doorstep pickup within 2 business days.
4. **Warehouse Inspection**: Upon arrival at the fulfillment hub, items undergo quality inspection within 24 to 48 hours.
5. **Refund Initiation**: Approved returns immediately trigger an automated refund to the original payment method.

## 4. Return Shipping Costs
- Defective, damaged, or incorrect items: **100% free return shipping** covered by company logistics.
- Customer preference / change of mind returns: Processed with standard courier pickup at zero additional charge for Gold and VIP tier members.
