# Enterprise Shipping & Delivery Policy

## 1. Delivery Timeframes & SLA
Orders are fulfilled from the nearest regional fulfillment center to ensure rapid delivery:
- **Standard Shipping**: Delivered within **3 to 5 business days** across all major metropolitan and tier-2 zones.
- **Express Delivery**: Delivered within **1 to 2 business days** for high-priority eligible pin codes.
- **Remote / Special Zones**: Delivered within 5 to 7 business days via national express couriers.

## 2. Order Tracking & Carrier Integration
- Every dispatched order is assigned an official Carrier Air Waybill (AWB) tracking number (e.g. `TRK425078349`).
- Tracking numbers are updated in the customer portal as soon as the order transitions to **"Shipped"** status.
- Real-time milestone updates: *Confirmed* ➔ *Processing* ➔ *Shipped* ➔ *Out for Delivery* ➔ *Delivered*.

## 3. Delivery Attempts & Rescheduling
- Couriers make up to **3 consecutive delivery attempts** before initiating a Return to Origin (RTO).
- Customers receive an automated SMS / WhatsApp notification on the morning of delivery with an OTP verification requirement.
- If you are unavailable, you can request delivery rescheduling through the AI support assistant before the 3rd attempt.

## 4. Shipping Address Modifications
- Address modifications are strictly permitted while an order status is **"Processing"**.
- Once an order changes to **"Shipped"** or **"Out for Delivery"**, address re-routing is locked for fraud prevention.
