# Enterprise Product FAQ & Customer Help Guide

## 1. Checking Product Pricing & Inventory
- **Real-Time Stock Verification**: Ask the AI Support Agent *"Is PROD0005 in stock?"* or search the **Product Catalog** tab to view live stock quantities and pricing in Indian Rupees (₹).
- **Price Transparency**: All displayed catalog prices are all-inclusive of applicable GST and standard transit charges.

## 2. Invoices & Tax Receipts
- **Download Invoices**: Tax-compliant commercial invoices with GSTIN breakdown are available for every confirmed order.
- Invoices are automatically attached to the order tracking view once the payment status is marked **"Paid"**.

## 3. Order Item Modification & Additions
- To add or remove items from an active order, please submit a cancellation request while status is "Processing", and place a new consolidated order with the updated item quantities.
- Once an order is in "Shipped" status, individual line items cannot be modified in transit.

## 4. Bulk Enterprise Orders & Corporate Procurement
- Enterprise procurement accounts ordering 50+ units qualify for tiered volume discounts.
- Open a support ticket under issue type **"Product query"** mentioning the SKU code and required quantities to connect with our B2B procurement division.
