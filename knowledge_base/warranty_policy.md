# Enterprise Hardware & Product Warranty Policy

## 1. Manufacturer Warranty Scope
All electronic and hardware items sold through our platform carry an official manufacturer warranty ranging from **6 to 24 months** as specified on the product datasheet:
- **Warranty Start Date**: Calculated deterministically from the confirmed **Order Date**.
- **Coverage Type**: Comprehensive hardware replacement or repair for internal component failures, power supply issues, screen defects, and factory defects.

## 2. Inclusions vs. Exclusions
### Covered Under Warranty:
- Motherboard, processor, sensor, or electronic circuit malfunctions.
- Battery failures exceeding normal capacity degradation within the warranty duration.
- Factory assembly and soldering defects.

### Excluded from Warranty:
- Physical damage from drops, heavy impact, or structural crushing.
- Liquid ingress, water submersion, or corrosion.
- Unauthorized disassembly, third-party repair modifications, or tampered warranty seals.

## 3. How to Claim Warranty
1. Check your product SKU in the AI Chat Studio (e.g. *"What is the warranty on PROD0020?"*).
2. The agent automatically retrieves your purchase date and calculates active warranty status.
3. If valid, open a support ticket under issue type **"Damaged item"** or **"Product query"** to schedule an authorized service center pickup.
